## Abstract

An affordance is a possibility for action that the environment offers an agent, defined by the relation between the agent's abilities and the world's properties. HCI borrowed the term from Gibson's ecological psychology and changed it, and it sits within a broader embodied view that meaning comes from acting in a physical and social world.

> **From the reading list:** Meaning comes from acting in a physical and social world, not from symbols in the head. Gibson's ecological psychology is the source of “affordance,” which HCI borrowed and changed.

## History

- 1979 — Gibson defines affordances in The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception.
- 1988 — Norman brings 'affordance' into design, using it for perceived action possibilities.
- 1991 — Gaver separates perceptible, hidden and false affordances and discusses sequential and nested ones.
- 2001 — Dourish grounds embodied interaction in phenomenology, joining tangible and social computing.

## Core concepts

- **Affordance (Gibson)** — A real relation between an animal and its environment that makes an action possible, whether or not it is perceived.
- **Perceived affordance** — Norman's design usage: the action possibilities a person believes an object offers.
- **Hidden and false affordances** — Gaver's terms for affordances that exist but are not perceptible, and apparent ones that do not exist.
- **Direct perception** — Gibson's claim that the environment's structure is picked up from the ambient array without inference.
- **Embodied interaction** — Dourish's term for interaction grounded in physical presence and social practice, drawing on phenomenology.

## Questions, controversies, and new developments

- **Controversies.** Norman's affordance (perceived action possibilities) diverged from Gibson's (real relations), causing years of confusion. Marr and Gibson disagreed on direct perception versus inference, and phenomenology is criticized as hard to operationalize.
- **Responses and new developments.** Norman added “signifiers” in the 2013 DOET. Kaptelinin and Nardi (CHI 2012) reframed affordances through mediated action, and embodied interaction became concrete design programs (Part IV).

## Broader connections

- **Influence.** Affordance learning is a research area in robotic grasping and computer vision. The ideas also shape VR and tangible interface design.

## Discussion questions

- Norman later added 'signifiers' to separate cues from affordances. Does that resolve the confusion with Gibson, or just rename it?
- What are the affordances of a text box for an AI model? Are any of them hidden or false in Gaver's sense?
- Dourish draws on phenomenology for design. What concrete design moves does Where the Action Is make possible that a cognitive account does not?
