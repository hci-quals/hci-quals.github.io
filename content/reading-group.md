## Before the meeting

Come in having read all **idea** readings and at least one **instantiation** reading of your choice.

## Structure of the meeting

- **20 min** — idea reading 1
- **20 min** — idea reading 2
- **20 min** — instantiation readings: 5 min writing down the idea, 15 min sharing

## Goal of the meeting

### For each idea reading, together

- What is the main idea?
- What is this trying to do?
- What is it saying?
- What kind of contribution is it?
- Why does this matter?
- How does this reading relate to, challenge, or shape the main idea?
- Your hot take on this reading

### For each instantiation reading, individually

- How does this relate to one of the idea readings? Try to be as specific as possible: what concepts map to each other?
- How does this reading relate to, challenge, or shape the main idea? *(together)*

## End of the meeting, together

- How else might these ideas be instantiated, in your area of expertise?
- What were the core concepts relevant to *computer science*?
- What are the common, *usable* concepts? Which researchers currently work on them?

## Afterwards

By the end of the meeting we should be able to produce a common document about these ideas, which will be put on the website.
